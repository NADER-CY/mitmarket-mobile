Interaction & Routing Guardrails

UX COMPLIANCE
bolt Zero Reload SPA State

History pushState caches live market state. Tab switches never re-render chart canvas or cancel active WebSocket feeds.

swipe_down Bottom Sheet Geometry

Execution slips use spring physics. Pull down > 80px dismisses sheet without accidental order triggers. Rubber band dampening applied.

warning Destructive 2-Step Gate

Withdrawal and Emergency Market Liquidation require secondary hold-to-confirm (800ms) to eliminate pocket presses.

phone_iphone Safe-Area Strict Compliance

All critical buttons pad minimum 34px from the base viewport to prevent conflict with iOS Home Indicator gestures.
